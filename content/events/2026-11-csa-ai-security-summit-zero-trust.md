**Save the date and register now. It's free.** The Cloud Security Alliance's AI Security Summit 2026 runs online over two days, November 18–19, and CSA Calgary encourages members and the wider community to sign up.

The summit addresses what CSA research calls the **AI Vulnerability Storm** — where non-human identities outnumber human workforces, autonomous AI agents execute multi-step workflows across enterprise APIs, and implicit network trust is no longer viable. Sessions focus on practical Zero Trust strategy for an AI-driven world: protecting human and machine identities, governing agentic systems, continuous verification, and securing the autonomous enterprise.

**Featured speakers & highlights:** Hear from Zero Trust and cloud security leaders including **John Kindervag** (Creator of Zero Trust & Chief Evangelist, Illumio), **Chase Cunningham** (Chief Strategy Officer, Demo-Force), **Dr. Georgianna "George" Shea** (Chief Technologist, FDD CCTI), and **Troy Leach** (Chief Strategy Officer, Cloud Security Alliance), alongside the annual **Startup Pitchapalooza 2026 ("Frontier-Ready")** competition.

**Why attend:** Zero Trust, non-human identity sprawl, and AI governance are the questions Calgary teams keep bringing to our events. This is a no-cost way to hear the CSA's global research community work through them, from wherever you are.

**Who should attend:** Security architects, cloud and identity engineers, GRC and AI governance leads, security leaders, and students interested in where cloud security is heading.

This event is hosted by the [Cloud Security Alliance](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/events/). Session times are listed on the registration page, and related research publications are available in our [Resources library](/resources/). After you register, join the chapter's LinkedIn group to compare notes with other Calgary attendees.

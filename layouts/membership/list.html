Global perspective, local momentum

Knowledge is everywhere. Context is closer to home.

CSA brings together people who advance cloud security through research, education, events, and community. Calgary is where that bigger conversation becomes personal, with local meetups and events that make it easier to find your people.

You might arrive with a problem to untangle, a new idea to test, or simple curiosity. You may leave having met a peer who challenges an assumption, opens a door, or changes what you do next.
